About Me

I am Dr. Hosenur Rahman Prodhani, an Assistant Professor of Statistics with a Ph.D. in Statistics from Assam University, Silchar.

My academic and research interests are primarily in statistical theory and its applications, with particular interests in distribution theory, reliability analysis, Bayesian analysis, regression analysis, and statistical computing.

My research work focuses on developing and studying probability distributions and their applications in statistical modelling, reliability and related areas.

I am also interested in statistical computing and the development of computational tools that support statistical analysis and research.

Research & Academic Profile

My research focuses on statistical theory and its applications. My major research interests include:

Distribution Theory

Development and study of probability distributions and their statistical properties.

Reliability Analysis

Statistical methods for reliability, lifetime and survival analysis.

Bayesian Analysis

Bayesian methods and statistical inference for modelling and estimation.

Regression Analysis

Statistical modelling and regression-based methods for analysing relationships among variables.

Statistical Computing

Computational methods and software tools for statistical analysis.
